# Computational BioMath Lab | TMU

### Mathematical Modeling • Computational Biology • Scientific Computing

A collaborative research space for mathematical modeling, computational biology, simulation, and reproducible scientific software.

## About

The Computational BioMath Lab at Tarbiat Modares University brings together students and researchers working on mathematical modeling, biological systems, scientific computing, and reproducible research workflows.

Our projects span theory, simulation, algorithmic development, and research-oriented software.

## Research Areas

- Mathematical modeling
- Computational biology
- Dynamical systems
- Network and graph-based modeling
- Scientific computing
- Biological simulation
- Data analysis and visualization
- Reproducible research workflows

## What You Will Find Here

- Research repositories
- Publication support materials
- Student projects
- Teaching and workshop resources
- Shared lab tools and templates
